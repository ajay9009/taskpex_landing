Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

TaskPex ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the TaskPex mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Device information (device ID, OS version, model)
  • Usage data (tasks completed, coins earned, game history)
  • Payment details for reward withdrawals (UPI ID)
  • Referral code and invite data

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the TaskPex service
  • To process reward redemptions and payouts
  • To prevent fraud and ensure fair play
  • To send app notifications and updates (with your permission)
  • To track referral rewards and milestones

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers (Firebase, payment gateways, AdMob) solely to operate the app. We comply with legal obligations when required.

4. Advertising

TaskPex uses Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures (Firebase Firestore) to protect your data. We regularly review our security practices.

6. Children's Privacy

TaskPex is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.
