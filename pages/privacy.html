Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025 · Package: com.app.taskpex

TaskPex ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the TaskPex mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device information (device ID, OS version, model)
  • Usage data (tasks completed, coins earned, game history)
  • Payment details for reward withdrawals (UPI ID)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the TaskPex service
  • To process reward redemptions and payouts
  • To prevent fraud and ensure fair play
  • To send app notifications and updates (with your permission)

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers (Firebase, payment gateways) solely to operate the app. We comply with legal obligations when required.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Firebase Firestore is used as our secure backend database.

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support@taskpex.com.

6. Contact
